# Image Attribution

**Every photograph below is a temporary stock placeholder. None shows BAMBOOS, its facilities, its staff or its events.** Each is labelled "Illustrative" on the site, and `npm run check:launch` blocks launch until all are replaced with real, licensed BAMBOOS photography.

Stock lives in `src/assets/media/stock/`. Real BAMBOOS photographs must go in a separate folder, `src/assets/media/bamboos/`, so the two can never be confused.

## Licence

All images come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). On 5 November 2026 each photo's own page was checked: its structured data declares that licence, and none is flagged as a premium Unsplash+ image.

In summary, the licence allows free commercial use and modification without required attribution. It doesn't allow selling the photos unaltered or compiling them into a competing service. Credits are given here as good practice.

**Not legal advice.** The licence covers the photographer's copyright, and Unsplash doesn't guarantee releases for people, private property or trademarks shown in a photo. Some images show identifiable interiors of real buildings, and hands of real people. Review the current licence, and these images, before any public use.

## Processing

Downloaded from Unsplash, resized to at most 1800 or 2400 px on the long edge, colour-graded to one brand look (reduced saturation, warm highlights, forest-tinted shadows), compressed with mozjpeg (quality 76), and stripped of metadata. Registry: `src/content/stock-images.ts`.

## Replacing a stock image

1. Add the real, licensed photo to `src/assets/media/bamboos/`.
2. Use it in `src/content/`, with an accurate alt text and no `illustrative` flag.
3. When nothing references a stock file, delete it and its row here.

**Why permission is unconfirmed.** BookMyMandap's [terms](https://bookmymandap.in/customer-terms-and-conditions) say vendors keep ownership of what they upload, and the listing grants nobody else a right to reuse it. **Confirm these with BAMBOOS before approving any photo:**

1. Who owns each image, and written permission to reuse it on this website.
2. That each image shows the real venue as it is. The files carry no camera metadata, their sizes and original filenames resemble image-generator output, and the two entrance images show different signs and planting. They may be AI-generated or heavily AI-enhanced, which could mislead visitors about the venue. This is an observation, not a finding.
3. Original, higher-resolution files. The largest is 1774 pixels wide, which looks soft on full-screen sections of large displays.
4. Whether the main auditorium image, with its deity idol, should be used at all.

**To approve a photo:** set its `status` to `"approved"` in `src/config/venue-photos.ts`, stop ignoring its file in `.gitignore`, and record who gave permission and when in the table above.

**Listing facts are not published.** The listing mentions an auditorium and open lawn, a capacity of up to 600, dressing rooms, electricity backup, parking and a street address. These are third-party claims, so they stay unpublished until BAMBOOS confirms them. A photograph doesn't confirm a facility either.
